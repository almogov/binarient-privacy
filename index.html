Privacy Policy – Business Manager

Business Manager is an internal tool used only by Binarient to manage its own expense invoices. It accesses only the Gmail accounts of the business owner, with the owner's consent, to read invoice emails and forward them to the company's accountant.

No data is shared with third parties, sold, or used for advertising. Data is stored on the company's own server.

Contact: almog@binarient.com
